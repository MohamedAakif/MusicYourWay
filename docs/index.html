Privacy Policy for Music Your Way

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

Music Your Way ("the App") is a local and offline Android music player developed by Arsignal Labs.

This Privacy Policy explains how Music Your Way handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Access

Music Your Way is designed to operate primarily on your device.

To provide its core functionality, the App may access audio files and related media information stored on your device, including:

  • Song titles
  • Artist names
  • Album names
  • Audio file locations
  • Other available music metadata

The App uses Android's MediaStore system to discover music available on your device.

The App does not upload your music files or music metadata to our servers.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Music Your Way stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features, including:

  • Tags assigned to songs
  • Playlists
  • Favorite songs
  • Play counts
  • Last-played timestamps
  • Folder scanning preferences
  • Playback and mode-related preferences

This information is stored locally using the App's local database and is not transmitted to a server operated by us.

3. Personal Information

Music Your Way does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or password to use the App.

We do not operate a user account system or maintain a server-side user profile for the App.

4. Google Play Billing

Music Your Way offers optional Pro subscriptions through Google Play Billing.

  • Monthly: $2 per month
  • Yearly: $20 per year

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Music Your Way does not collect or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

The App receives purchase information from Google Play that is necessary to determine whether Pro features have been purchased and to provide the corresponding functionality.

For information about how Google handles payment and purchase information, please refer to Google's applicable privacy policies and Google Play terms.

5. Data Sharing

Music Your Way does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The App does not operate a backend server that receives your music library, tags, playlists, favorites, or listening history.

Google Play Billing processes purchases through Google Play as described above.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Music Your Way does not contain advertising and does not use advertising services.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or behavioral tracking.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Music Your Way remains on your device until you delete it through the App's available functionality, clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the associated local data through Android.

Because the App does not maintain a server-side account or database containing your personal information, there is no server-side personal account data that we retain or need to delete.

Google Play purchase records are handled by Google according to Google's applicable policies.

8. Data Security

Music Your Way is designed to keep its local application data within Android's application storage and does not transmit the user's music library, tags, playlists, favorites, or listening history to our servers.

Reasonable measures are used within the App to protect locally stored application data. However, no software or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Music Your Way is a general-purpose music player and is not specifically directed toward children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published at this privacy-policy URL and will include a revised effective date.
